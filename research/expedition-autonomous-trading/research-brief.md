# Research Brief: Autonomous Self-Funding Trading Agent

## Date: 2026-03-09
## Project: MIDGE

### Problem Statement

AI agents are already trading autonomously on prediction markets, opening their own crypto wallets, and earning enough to pay for their own compute costs. MIDGE has a powerful pattern recognition and intelligence layer but currently only surfaces alerts for human decision-making. Guiding Light wants MIDGE to become a fully autonomous, self-funding trader — turn her on and walk away while she generates returns.

### Expected Outcome

A clear roadmap showing: (1) how existing autonomous AI traders actually work (infrastructure, execution, funding), (2) exactly where MIDGE stands today versus what's needed, (3) what markets MIDGE should master first, (4) how to achieve stealth (MIDGE must be an unknown, undetectable market presence), and (5) what cross-domain signals give MIDGE an edge nobody else has.

The end state: MIDGE runs autonomously, perfects one market domain, generates consistent returns, funds her own compute, then expands to the next domain. No human decisions required. Guiding Light turns MIDGE on and she works.

### Current State

MIDGE has:
- 30 data sources across 12 domains (insider trading, congressional activity, government contracts, energy, technical analysis, sentiment, crypto, macro, positioning, institutional, events, fundamentals)
- Convergence engine that stacks 3+ independent patterns for high-confidence alerts
- Thompson Sampling (Bayesian learning) that self-calibrates signal reliability
- Granger causality analysis (detects directional causal relationships between signals)
- Pattern Archaeology (reverse-engineers historical moves to find recurring templates)
- Hypothesis engine (generates, validates, promotes/retires trading hypotheses)
- Paper trading system (simulated trades with Kelly position sizing)
- Proven statistical edge: z=4.74, p<0.0001 (convergence alerts beat random by 2x)
- Daemon mode (runs continuously, 24/7 capable)

MIDGE lacks:
- Actual trade execution (no broker/exchange connection)
- Wallet/account management
- Autonomous decision-making (alerts go to humans, not to an execution engine)
- Self-funding loop (no mechanism to reinvest profits)
- Cross-market arbitrage capability
- Prediction market participation

### Project Direction

MIDGE is a recursive self-improving pattern discovery organism. Her core loop: discover pattern → backtest → register hypothesis → evaluate against reality → adjust weights → discover BETTER patterns → improve HOW she discovers. The vision is total financial independence through proprietary pattern recognition that continuously improves.

Key principle: MIDGE's edge is NOT speed. It's cross-domain pattern convergence — seeing insider trades + congressional activity + energy data + technical patterns converge on the same opportunity. This looks like an extremely well-informed human, not an algorithm.

### Constraints

- Mae's 8 Mathematical Laws govern all architecture (triadic connections, holon protocol, fractal self-similarity, autopoietic closure)
- MIDGE must remain stealthy — an unknown, undetectable market presence
- Master one domain before expanding to the next (depth before breadth)
- Pattern stacking is the core mechanism (not individual signal accuracy)
- All learning must be self-calibrating (no manual tuning by Guiding Light)

### Destructive Boundaries

- Do NOT recommend replacing MIDGE's pattern recognition architecture — it's proven and proprietary
- Do NOT recommend approaches that require Guiding Light to make trading decisions
- Do NOT recommend approaches that make MIDGE detectable as algorithmic
- The intelligence layer (Thompson, convergence, archaeology) stays — the question is what wraps around it

### Research Angles

**Team 1: Autonomous AI Trading Infrastructure**
How do current autonomous AI traders actually work? Polymarket bots, crypto trading agents, the 13,000 agents with wallets. What's the actual tech stack? How do they connect intelligence to execution? What APIs, protocols, and wallet systems do they use? Focus on what's real and working TODAY, not hypothetical.

**Team 2: Self-Funding Mechanisms & Market Selection**
How do AI agents fund their own compute? What are the actual P&L numbers from Polymarket bots? Which markets are most accessible for an AI to start generating returns with minimal capital? Compare: prediction markets, crypto spot/futures, traditional equities, forex, options. What's the easiest first market for MIDGE to master and generate consistent returns?

**Team 3: MIDGE Gap Analysis & Execution Bridge**
Given MIDGE's current capabilities (pattern recognition, convergence alerts, paper trading), what specific components are needed to bridge from "surfaces alerts" to "executes trades autonomously"? What broker/exchange APIs support programmatic trading? What's the minimum viable execution layer? How do you go from paper trades to real money with proper risk management?

**Team 4: Stealth & Anti-Detection**
How do you trade algorithmically without being detected? What patterns trigger algorithmic trading detection systems? Which markets have the least surveillance? How do you make AI trades look like informed human trades? What order types, sizing strategies, and timing patterns avoid detection? Specifically: does MIDGE's cross-domain approach naturally evade detection because it mimics informed human behavior?

**Team 5: Cross-Domain Signal Mastery & Competitive Moat**
Beyond financial data: weather patterns, logistics/shipping, social media, political events, supply chains, satellite imagery, foot traffic. What cross-domain signals are proven predictive of market moves? What are existing AI traders NOT using that MIDGE could exploit? What's the highest-signal cross-domain correlation that nobody is currently stacking? This is MIDGE's potential moat — investigate what makes it defensible.

### Team Size: 5

Five distinct, non-overlapping research angles covering infrastructure, economics, engineering, operations security, and competitive advantage. This is a major strategic decision — MIDGE's entire future direction depends on these findings.

### Failed Approaches

- None specific to autonomous trading (MIDGE hasn't attempted execution yet)
- Previous research found MIDGE's cross-domain convergence is a genuine structural moat — no competitor does it (expedition-competitive-edge, 2026-03-05)
- Individual signal accuracy is NOT the path — pattern STACKING is (Guiding Light corrected this twice)
