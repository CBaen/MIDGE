# Research Brief: Inhabitant Culture
## Date: 2026-03-08
## Project: MIDGE

### Problem Statement
"I expect an entire culture of inhabitants. Who's going to design and build them all so they can be put to work and begin the real collaborative research?"

MIDGE has 29 biological systems wired to market channels — they have jobs. But they don't have lives. They react when the main loop ticks. They don't wake up on their own, pursue their own goals, or coordinate with each other autonomously. The Paperclip proposal envisioned 50+ autonomous agents with heartbeats, budgets, and org charts — but Paperclip itself was rejected (Law 6: autopoietic closure demands internal governance, not an external platform).

The question: How do we build a culture of inhabitants from within — entities with intrinsic drives, independent schedules, and emergent coordination — without violating the mathematical laws?

### Expected Outcome
When you look at MIDGE running, you see a living ecosystem. Systems wake up on their own schedules. Some are curious and chase partial signals. Some are cautious and patrol for threats. They coordinate through pheromone trails and quorum sensing, not through a central dispatcher. New inhabitants can emerge when needed (morphogenesis) and retire when worn out (senescence). The culture produces collaborative research — converging on inevitabilities through collective intelligence, not formula.

### Project Direction
MIDGE is an inevitability surfacer — a planet, not a single organism. Every component should be alive with intrinsic purpose. The convergence engine doesn't care what signals are — when independent domains converge, that's inevitability forming. The ecosystem needs to perceive across all domains simultaneously through distributed autonomous attention.

### Constraints
- 8 Mathematical Laws (especially Law 6 autopoietic closure — no external orchestration)
- Law 1: No bare dyads — every connection needs a witness
- Law 2: Triadic generator — minimum 3 for stability
- Law 5: Stem Cell — one universal class, specialization via configuration
- Zero regressions — existing 4,536 tests must pass
- No external control planes — Paperclip rejected. Everything must be internal.
- Advisory enforcement — triads observe/report, never block
- Mesa 3.4 foundation, pure Python infrastructure

### Destructive Boundaries
- Do NOT redesign the convergence alerter, Thompson sampler, or pattern archaeology
- Do NOT remove the Mesa step loop (it's the organism's heartbeat)
- Do NOT introduce external dependencies that create single points of failure
- Do NOT propose paid platforms or services (except minimal API costs)
- Do NOT break the 33-layer bootstrap order

### Research Angles
Team 1 — Intrinsic Drive Architecture: How do you give software entities autonomous motivation? BDI agents, drive-based architectures, homeostatic regulation as motivation, biological drive generation.

Team 2 — Emergent Culture & Coordination: How do multi-agent systems develop shared norms, collective intelligence, and emergent coordination without central control? Stigmergy, quorum sensing, swarm intelligence, artificial societies.

Team 3 — Self-Governance Within Autopoietic Closure: How do you achieve budgets, heartbeats, accountability, and org charts purely through internal mechanisms? Biological resource allocation, immune privilege, endocrine feedback, metabolic budgeting.

Team 4 — Autonomous Scheduling & Scale: How do you run 50+ semi-independent entities within Python/Mesa? Coroutines, asyncio, thread pools, actor models, step-to-wallclock migration.

### Team Size: 4
Four independent angles that don't overlap. Each addresses a different dimension: motivation (why they act), coordination (how they work together), governance (who ensures fairness), execution (how it runs on hardware).

### Failed Approaches
- Paperclip platform — Rejected for violating Law 6 (external governance). Vision right, implementation wrong.
- Pure step-based activation — Current state. Reactive participants, not autonomous inhabitants.
- Shedding bio systems — Guiding Light overruled: activate them, don't shed them.
